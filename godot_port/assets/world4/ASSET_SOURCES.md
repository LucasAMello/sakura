# World 4 asset provenance

All PNGs preserve their original dimensions and pixels and replace Allegro's RGB `(255, 0, 255)` mask color with alpha. Godot uses nearest-neighbor filtering.

`bubble.png` and `splash_1.png` through `splash_3.png` are direct conversions of `bolhas.bmp`, `splash.bmp`, `splash2.bmp`, and `splash3.bmp` from `old/5 jogos new sakura`.
