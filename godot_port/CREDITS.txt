SAKURA GODOT PORT

Original game and artwork: Sakura, 2008 archive
Canonical preservation source: old/2 Joguito
Godot port: Sakura Preservation Project

Engine: Godot Engine 4.7.2
MIDI renderer: FluidSynth 2.6.0
General MIDI soundfont: MuseScore General

The port preserves the original 640×480 pixel artwork, campaign maps, music compositions, sound effects, and gameplay structure. Original game content remains subject to its original ownership.
